Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

ACO ("Autonomous Content Operations", "we") is a desktop application that helps creators produce short-form videos and plan content for channels they own or manage. This policy explains what data ACO accesses, how it is used, where it is stored, and how you can remove it.

Data ACO accesses

Google and YouTube

When you connect a YouTube channel, ACO requests read-only access through Google OAuth:

  • youtube.readonly — your channel's name, handle and public statistics (subscribers, views, video list).
  • yt-analytics.readonly — your channel's analytics reports, such as views, watch time, average view duration, audience retention and viewer countries.

ACO cannot upload, edit or delete videos, comments or settings with these permissions.

Meta (Facebook)

When you connect Facebook Pages you manage, ACO reads the page list, page engagement and page insights so it can report how your content performs.

How the data is used

ACO uses this data only to show you how your own content performs and to suggest and plan future content for the same channels. It is not used for advertising, it is not sold, and it is not used to train AI models.

Storage and security

OAuth tokens and analytics data are stored locally on your own computer, inside the ACO application folder. ACO does not send them to servers operated by us. Requests go directly from your computer to Google and Meta over encrypted HTTPS connections.

Sharing

We do not share, sell or transfer data received from Google, YouTube or Meta to any third party. AI services used to create content (for example scripts, images and narration) receive only the content you are producing, never your account tokens or analytics data.

Google API Services User Data Policy

ACO's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube API Services

ACO uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube channel you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

  • Revoke ACO's Google access at any time at Google security settings.
  • Remove ACO from Facebook in Facebook Business Integrations.
  • Uninstalling ACO or deleting its data folder removes the stored tokens and analytics from your computer. Stored YouTube analytics are refreshed or deleted within 30 days, as the YouTube API Services policies require.

Children

ACO is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We will post any change to this policy on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Autonomous Content Operations

ACO is a desktop content studio. It plans, writes, illustrates, narrates and reviews short-form videos for your own channels, then reads your channel analytics to decide what to make next.

How it works

  1. 1. ResearchFinds topics people care about right now and checks the facts.
  2. 2. WriteDrafts an original script in your channel's locked voice and style.
  3. 3. CreateGenerates scenes, narration and captions, each one reviewed before use.
  4. 4. LearnReads your own YouTube and Facebook statistics to plan the next video.

Your channels, your control

ACO connects only to channels and pages you own or manage, and only with read-only analytics access. Connection keys stay on your own computer. You decide what is uploaded and published.

Read exactly what ACO accesses and how it is handled in the Privacy Policy.
